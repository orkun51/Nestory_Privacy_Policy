Privacy Policy for Nestory

Last updated: January 2026

Nestory ("we", "our", or "the app") is a home companion app that helps you manage finances, kitchen inventory, household assets, and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and the name you provide.
  • Finance data: Transactions you add (amount, category, date, notes).
  • Kitchen data: Inventory items and shopping list entries you create.
  • Household data: Assets you register (name, category, condition, dates, notes).
  • Reminders: Titles, descriptions, due dates, and types you set.
  • Preferences: Language, currency, and app settings you choose.
  • Household membership: If you join or create a household, we store your membership and approval status so you can share data with other approved members.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app’s features (finance, kitchen, household, reminders).
  • To sync your data across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To allow shared access within a household after the owner approves members.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted by security rules so that only you (and, for shared household data, approved household members) can read or write it.

4. Third-Party Services

We use:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication and database storage. Google’s privacy practices apply to the processing of data by Firebase: Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and data by signing out and ceasing use, or by contacting us to request deletion.
  • You can manage or leave a household from the app’s Settings.
  • You can change your preferences (e.g. language, currency) at any time in the app.

6. Children

Nestory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can open an issue or contact the maintainers via the Nestory app’s support or the repository: Nestory_Privacy_Policy.
