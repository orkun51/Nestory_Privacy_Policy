Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Nestory ("we", "our", or "the app") is a home companion app that helps you manage finances, kitchen inventory, household assets, and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account: Email address and display name you provide when signing up.
  • Finance data: Transactions you add (amount, category, date, optional notes).
  • Kitchen data: Inventory items and shopping list entries you create.
  • Household assets: Items you register (name, category, condition, purchase date, warranty, notes).
  • Reminders: Titles, descriptions, due dates, and reminder types you set.
  • Preferences: Language, currency, dark mode, and notification settings.
  • Household membership: If you join or create a shared household, we store your membership role and approval status.
  • Push notification token: A device token used solely to deliver your scheduled local reminders. We do not send marketing notifications.
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active Nestory Pro subscription, verified via RevenueCat. We never store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync the app's core features across your devices.
  • To enable household data sharing with members you have approved.
  • To deliver scheduled local reminder notifications on your device.
  • To determine whether to show advertisements. Free users see banner ads; Nestory Pro subscribers never see ads.
  • To verify and manage your Pro subscription through RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication). All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest. Firestore security rules ensure that only you and approved household members can access your data.

4. Advertising (Free Users)

Free users see banner advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and, where consent is given, advertising IDs to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Nestory Pro subscribers see no advertisements.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and all data by contacting us (see Section 10).
  • You can manage or leave a household in the app's Settings screen at any time.
  • You can update your preferences (language, currency, notifications) in the app at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's privacy settings.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the GDPR including rights to access, correct, restrict, or erase your personal data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Children

Nestory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For questions, data requests, or account deletion requests, please contact us via the app's support channel or the repository: github.com/orkun51/Nestory_Privacy_Policy.
